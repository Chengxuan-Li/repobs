# Tutorial

Five chapters, from installing repobs to arranging it the way you work. Each takes a few minutes; follow them in order the first time.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

1.  [Install and add your projects](start.md)

    Install repobs, start it, add your repositories and folders, and check that your agents are found.

2.  [Read the dashboard](dashboard.md)

    Branches, commits and the timeline; zooming, panning and the legend.

3.  [Follow your agents](agents.md)

    Statuses, details, what each agent did, spawned agents and agents' plans.

4.  [Review and take notes](review.md)

    Review states, notes and checklists, filters, hiding agents, and rules that do it for you.

5.  [Arrange your workspace](workspace.md)

    Views, panels, commands, settings, and saving your setup.

</div>
